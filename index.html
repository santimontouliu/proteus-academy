05 / SANTIAGO MONTOULIU VILLEGAS · FOUNDER

Teaching since 2014.
Depth at every level.

From high-school foundations to advanced Mathematics and the international Physics Olympiad arena.

My teaching journey began in 2014, when I returned to my former high school as a Physics Olympiad instructor and began teaching private students, mainly at high-school level. In 2015, as my own university studies deepened, I expanded into private instruction in university Mathematics and Physics.

From 2021 to 2023, I also taught at university. My teaching experience spans Mechanics, Electromagnetism, Calculus and Differential Equations, through to Geometry of Curves and Surfaces, Riemannian Geometry, Topology and Algebraic Topology.

Since 2023, I have worked privately with IB Diploma students; I added IGCSE instruction in 2025. Alongside Physics Olympiad training, I have served for eleven years on an organizing committee and participated in six Olympiads as an international jury member.

Proteus Academy, a venture under Proteus Analytics LLC, brings this experience together around a conviction developed through teaching: lasting progress begins when students understand ideas deeply enough to reason for themselves.

A wider teaching practice

My private IB teaching has included Physics, Mathematics Analysis and Approaches, Mathematics Applications and Interpretation, Chemistry, Business Management, and Environmental Systems and Societies. My IGCSE experience includes Mathematics, Physics, Combined Sciences, and environmental studies.

I have also taught high-school Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics, and English, and Basque through the Euskara Munduan program. This breadth informs an academy whose core focus is Mathematics and Physics.

Who is Proteus Academy for?

Ambitious students studying Mathematics or Physics at IB Diploma, IGCSE, A Level, or university level, and students seeking deeper enrichment through Scholars / Olympiad preparation. A diagnostic conversation helps establish fit.

What does “selective” mean?

We look for curiosity, willingness to practise, and a fit between the student’s goals and available expertise. Selectivity is about thoughtful support and capacity, not a promise of admission or an invented academic cutoff.

Are lessons online or in person?

Lessons are delivered online over Zoom or Google Meet, with a shared digital whiteboard so each step stays visible. Teaching is available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, and Basque. Session times and the platform are agreed during consultation.

Do you offer individual or small-group learning?

We offer individual online instruction and small-group Scholars / Olympiad sessions. Group formation, lesson length, schedule, and availability are confirmed before enrollment.

Can you guarantee a grade or exam result?

No. Learning depends on many factors, including prior preparation, independent practice, attendance, and assessment conditions. We focus on clear goals, thoughtful feedback, and evidence of understanding.

How much do programs cost?

Launch pricing is in USD: $95 for a 75-minute assessment; $360 for four 60-minute Private Mentorship sessions; $680 for eight 60-minute Excellence sessions over four weeks; and $960 for eight 60-minute Scholars sessions over four weeks. See mentorship packages and planned cohort prices. Final scope, any applicable taxes, payment and cancellation terms are confirmed in writing before enrollment.

Is Proteus affiliated with IB or Cambridge?

No official affiliation, endorsement, accreditation, or authorization is claimed. References to IB, IGCSE, and A Levels identify students’ study pathways. Proteus Academy is an independent education venture.

How can I apply or book a consultation?

Book a 15-minute consultation through Google Calendar (opens in a new tab), or email contact@useproteus.io. The inquiry form sends your details to our academy inbox through FormSubmit. It does not book a meeting or confirm enrollment. Students under 18 should involve a parent or guardian.
