About this website

Sending an inquiry does not create a contract, reserve a place, confirm admission, or book a consultation. The inquiry builder only prepares an email draft. You must send it through your own email application; the website does not confirm delivery.

Package payment

Mentorship packages are paid upfront, before the first session in the package. The agreed scope, price and payment instructions are provided in writing before enrollment. This website does not collect payments.

Individual-session cancellation and rescheduling

  • With at least 12 hours’ notice, an individual session may be rescheduled without charge, subject to availability.
  • With less than 12 hours’ notice, or if a student does not attend, the session counts as used.
  • If the instructor cancels, the student is offered a replacement session or a refund for that session.
  • Emergency exceptions may be considered at the academy’s discretion.

These scheduling rules do not determine refunds for unused package sessions. Package withdrawal, unused-session refunds and group-program arrangements will be agreed before enrollment.
