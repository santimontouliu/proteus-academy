Privacy notice — launch placeholder

Draft notice for this launch preview. This is not a finalized privacy policy. The academy must confirm its contact details, hosting arrangements, data practices, and applicable obligations before accepting admissions.

Inquiry form

When you submit an inquiry, your name, email address, role, study pathway, area of interest, learning goals, consent response, and the website address are sent over HTTPS to FormSubmit for delivery to contact@useproteus.io. FormSubmit may also process technical request information. Please avoid sensitive information, school records, identification documents, or health details. Students under 18 should involve a parent or guardian.

FormSubmit states that it retains form submissions for 30 days. The academy receives a copy through its email service. This website does not maintain a separate submissions database or store entries in browser storage. Your browser may retain form entries through its own autofill features. Academy inbox retention and deletion arrangements still need to be finalized.

Read the FormSubmit privacy terms (PDF, opens in a new tab) and submission documentation. A successful submission is not a booking, admission, or payment confirmation. The academy must activate its FormSubmit recipient address before email forwarding is fully enabled.

Email and consultation booking

Email links open your chosen mail application; sending a message shares its contents and your email address with the academy through the relevant email services. The consultation link opens Google Calendar in a new tab. Information you enter there is handled through Google’s booking service. No booking data is collected by this static website. Final retention and handling practices for inquiries and bookings must be documented before the privacy policy is finalized.

Interactive demonstration

The progress dashboard uses fictional data. The reflection exercise stays in the open dialog and is cleared when it closes. No real student record is connected.

Hosting and fonts

The hosting provider may process technical request information, including IP addresses and access logs. Google Fonts is requested to display the site’s typography; that request may disclose connection information to Google. This site adds no analytics or advertising scripts and sets no application cookies. Hosting, authentication, and external form/spam-prevention services may use their own cookies or technical request information.

Before admissions open

The final notice must identify the responsible business and a verified privacy contact, explain collected data and purposes, recipients and international transfers, retention, applicable rights and request procedures, and arrangements for students under 18. Any booking, payment, analytics, or form provider must be reflected here before use.

Contact

For academy inquiries, email contact@useproteus.io. Please avoid sending sensitive information in an initial inquiry. Students under 18 should involve a parent or guardian.
