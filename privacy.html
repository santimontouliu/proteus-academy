Privacy notice — launch placeholder

Draft notice for this launch preview. This is not a finalized privacy policy. The academy must confirm its contact details, hosting arrangements, data practices, and applicable obligations before accepting admissions.

This preview

The inquiry builder runs in your browser. It does not transmit entries to Proteus Academy, create an account, store entries in browser storage, or submit an application. It prepares a text file on your device containing the information you enter. Your browser may retain form entries through its own autofill features.

Email and consultation booking

Email links open your chosen mail application; sending a message shares its contents and your email address with the academy through the relevant email services. The consultation link opens Google Calendar in a new tab. Information you enter there is handled through Google’s booking service. No booking data is collected by this static website. Final retention and handling practices for inquiries and bookings must be documented before the privacy policy is finalized.

Interactive demonstration

The progress dashboard uses fictional data. The reflection exercise stays in the open dialog and is cleared when it closes. No real student record is connected.

Hosting and fonts

The hosting provider may process technical request information, including IP addresses and access logs. Google Fonts is requested to display the site’s typography; that request may disclose connection information to Google. This site adds no analytics or advertising scripts and sets no application cookies. Hosting or authentication services may use their own cookies.

Before admissions open

The final notice must identify the responsible business and a verified privacy contact, explain collected data and purposes, recipients and international transfers, retention, applicable rights and request procedures, and arrangements for students under 18. Any booking, payment, analytics, or form provider must be reflected here before use.

Contact

For academy inquiries, email contact@useproteus.io. Please avoid sending sensitive information in an initial inquiry. Students under 18 should involve a parent or guardian.
